## Creating a CV

### 📂 Evidence
- Link to CV Markdown on GitHub
- ![CV preview](/images/CV_sub_image.png)
- ![CV preview 2](/images/CV_sub_image_2.png)

### 💭 Reflection (STAR Technique)

#### Situation
For my Project Presentation module, we were tasked with creating a professional CV tailored specifically to the tech industry. This formative assessment, titled 'Create a CV', was designed to equip us with the fundamental knowledge and practical skills needed to present ourselves effectively to potential employers in the competitive technology sector. During the briefing sessions, we received comprehensive instruction on the primary purposes of a CV, including how it serves as a marketing tool to showcase our unique value proposition to hiring managers. We explored the appropriate structuring of CVs, learning about different formats such as chronological, functional, and combination styles, and discussed which approach would best serve our individual career goals and experience levels. Additionally, we delved into the critical components that should be included within the CV itself, ranging from professional experience and technical skills to educational background, certifications, personal projects, and relevant contact information. This foundational knowledge helped me understand that a CV is not merely a list of past experiences, but rather a strategic document that needs to be carefully crafted to align with specific job requirements and industry expectations.

#### Task
My primary objective in creating this CV was to effectively highlight all of my accumulated skills, completed projects, and educational achievements in a manner that clearly demonstrates alignment with the current job opportunities available in the software engineering field. I needed to strike a delicate balance between being comprehensive enough to showcase my full range of capabilities while remaining concise and focused on the most relevant information that would resonate with recruiters and hiring managers. The challenge was to differentiate myself from other candidates by emphasizing my unique combination of technical proficiencies, problem-solving abilities, and soft skills that make me well-suited for roles in software development. I also recognized the importance of making my CV adaptable, ensuring it could be easily tailored for different positions while maintaining a consistent professional brand throughout.

#### Action
To accomplish this task, I leveraged a professional Canva template that provided a solid foundation while allowing for extensive customization to incorporate my personal branding and ensure the final product reflected my professional identity. I carefully selected a template with a clean, modern aesthetic that would appeal to tech industry recruiters while maintaining excellent readability and visual hierarchy. Throughout the customization process, I paid particular attention to typography, spacing, and the strategic use of color to draw attention to key sections without overwhelming the reader. Beyond just the visual design, I invested significant effort in crafting compelling content for each section, using action verbs and quantifiable achievements wherever possible to demonstrate tangible impact. Furthermore, I embraced GitHub and Markdown as essential tools in my professional toolkit, using these platforms not only to host my CV but also to create comprehensive README files and develop an online portfolio. This dual approach served multiple purposes: it demonstrated my technical competency with version control systems and documentation practices that are fundamental in software development, while also providing recruiters with easy access to my work and evidence of my ability to communicate technical concepts clearly through written documentation.

#### Reflection / Result
Through this comprehensive CV creation process, I have developed a newfound confidence in my ability to effectively showcase my professional potential to prospective employers. The structured approach to CV development has transformed the way I think about self-presentation in professional contexts, helping me recognize that a CV is a living document that requires regular updates and refinement rather than a static artifact. I now maintain my CV in a way that keeps it continuously up-to-date with my latest skills, projects, and experiences, ensuring that I'm always prepared to respond to unexpected opportunities in the software engineering field. The skills I developed through this process extend beyond just CV writing; I've gained valuable insights into personal branding, the importance of tailoring application materials to specific roles, and the strategic thinking required to position myself competitively in the job market. Moreover, the practice of documenting my work through GitHub and Markdown has become an integral part of my professional workflow, enabling me to build a portfolio that tells a coherent story about my growth as a developer and demonstrates my commitment to continuous learning and improvement in the fast-evolving tech industry.

This assessment was strategically positioned within our curriculum to help us understand not just what we can do technically, but who we are as professionals and how our inherent personality traits influence our work style and career satisfaction. The timing of this assessment was particularly valuable as it came at a point in our education where we were beginning to consider our transition from academic life to professional careers, making self-awareness crucial for making informed decisions about job opportunities and career paths. The assessment examined various dimensions of personality, including our preferences for social interaction, decision-making styles, approaches to problem-solving, and responses to stress and change.

#### Task
The overarching goal of this personality assessment was to cultivate greater self-awareness regarding my inherent strengths, potential blind spots, and natural preferences in professional settings. I needed to honestly engage with the assessment process, reflect critically on the results, and then thoughtfully map these personality insights onto realistic career expectations and opportunities within the software engineering field. This wasn't merely an academic exercise; it required genuine introspection about who I am, how I prefer to work, and what types of environments and roles would allow me to thrive and contribute most effectively. The challenge was to view the results not as limiting labels but as valuable insights that could inform my career decisions and help me position myself in roles where my natural tendencies would be assets rather than obstacles.

#### Action
I approached the formative assessment with seriousness and honesty, taking time to carefully consider each question rather than rushing through to reach a result. After receiving my personality profile, I engaged in deep reflection on how the identified traits manifest in my daily life, academic work, and interactions with others. I considered specific examples from group projects, independent work, and various life experiences that either confirmed or challenged the assessment's findings. I paid particular attention to understanding how my personality traits affect my teamwork dynamics, including how I communicate with collaborators, handle conflicts, respond to feedback, and contribute to group decision-making processes. Additionally, I reflected on how these traits influence my professional behavior, including my work style preferences, motivation triggers, and the types of professional challenges that energize versus drain me.

The assessment revealed that I possess an ambivert personality profile, exhibiting characteristics of both introversion and extroversion depending on the context and my energy levels. This finding resonated strongly with my lived experience, as I recognized that while I genuinely enjoy collaborative brainstorming sessions and team interactions when working on meaningful projects, I also require substantial periods of independent, focused work to process information deeply and produce my best work. I discovered that I particularly thrive when engaged in independent, creative work that allows me to explore problems deeply and develop innovative solutions without constant external input or interruption. The assessment also highlighted my strong preference for flexible working environments that provide autonomy while still offering opportunities for collaboration and knowledge sharing when needed. These insights have proven remarkably valuable in understanding which software engineering roles would be the best fit for my personality; positions that offer a balance of independent development work, creative problem-solving opportunities, and periodic team collaboration align perfectly with my natural tendencies and work style preferences. This self-knowledge has empowered me to ask more informed questions during interviews, seek out companies with cultures that match my values, and ultimately position myself for long-term career satisfaction rather than just immediate employment.

## Interview Skills

### 📂 Evidence
- ![Interview skills](/images/image-4.png)

### 💭 Reflection (STAR Technique)

#### Situation
The interview skills development component of my professional preparation program was founded on the recognition that interviewing represents one of the most critical competencies for career success, serving as the primary mechanism through which companies evaluate candidates' suitability for positions. Understanding that even highly qualified candidates can fail to secure positions due to poor interview performance, I recognized the vital importance of developing strong interviewing skills alongside my technical capabilities. The program emphasized that interviewing is fundamentally a multifaceted communication exercise that requires simultaneously conveying competence, demonstrating cultural fit, building rapport, asking intelligent questions, and managing nervousness—all while accurately representing one's authentic self and genuine interest in the opportunity. Through structured instruction, I learned that interview success isn't primarily about having perfect answers but rather about demonstrating thought processes, problem-solving approaches, and interpersonal skills that align with what the organization seeks.

#### Task
The primary tasks associated with developing interview proficiency involved cultivating the confidence and practical skills necessary to present myself effectively during the high-pressure, high-stakes context of professional interviews. This encompassed multiple dimensions of preparation: researching prospective employers thoroughly to understand their products, services, culture, values, and recent developments; deeply analyzing job descriptions to identify key requirements, priorities, and potential talking points; preparing compelling narratives about my background, projects, and experiences that would resonate with interviewers; developing thoughtful responses to both common interview questions and challenging behavioral scenarios; and practicing techniques for clearly articulating my strengths while addressing perceived weaknesses or gaps in experience honestly and constructively. The challenge was to develop interview approaches that felt authentic rather than rehearsed, demonstrating genuine enthusiasm and interest while maintaining professional composure.

#### Action
To build interview competency, I engaged extensively with mock interview exercises that simulated realistic interview conditions and pressure levels, allowing me to practice responding to various question types including technical assessments, behavioral scenarios, and situational judgment questions. These practice sessions proved invaluable for identifying and addressing nervous habits, verbal tics, and areas where my responses lacked clarity or focus. I studied and practiced structured response frameworks, particularly the STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) method for behavioral questions and the CAR (Challenge, Action, Result) method, learning how these frameworks help organize thinking and deliver responses that are both comprehensive and concise. Beyond just preparing answers, I developed skills for engaging interviewers in genuine conversation, asking thoughtful questions that demonstrated research and interest, and reading social cues to adjust my approach dynamically. I also worked on practical aspects of interview presentation, including appropriate professional attire, confident body language, eye contact, and voice modulation to project competence and enthusiasm.

#### Result
As a result of this comprehensive preparation and practice, I now approach interview scenarios feeling significantly better prepared and equipped to succeed in competitive hiring processes. I've developed confidence in my ability to articulate my value proposition clearly and compellingly, using concrete examples from my projects and experiences to demonstrate relevant capabilities. I understand how to effectively employ structured response methods like the CAR framework to ensure my answers are organized, relevant, and memorable rather than rambling or tangential. I've learned the importance of thorough company research, not just for answering questions but for asking intelligent questions that demonstrate genuine interest and help me evaluate whether the opportunity aligns with my career goals and values. Perhaps most importantly, I've developed the metacognitive awareness to reflect on interview performances constructively, identifying what worked well and what could be improved for future opportunities rather than simply feeling anxious or disappointed after challenging interviews. This growth mindset approach to interviewing has transformed it from a dreaded ordeal into a valuable learning opportunity where each interview, regardless of outcome, contributes to my ongoing development as a professional.

The mock interviews covered various interview formats including one-on-one behavioral interviews, technical problem-solving sessions, and panel interviews, ensuring comprehensive preparation for diverse interview scenarios we might encounter in our job searches.

My specific task was to approach this mock interview with the same level of seriousness, preparation, and professionalism that I would bring to an actual job interview with a company I genuinely wanted to join. This meant conducting thorough preliminary research on the hypothetical company and role provided in the simulation scenario, understanding their products, services, competitive positioning, and organizational culture. I had to ready myself to respond effectively to both technical questions that would assess my programming knowledge, problem-solving abilities, and understanding of software development best practices, as well as behavioral questions designed to evaluate my teamwork skills, conflict resolution approaches, leadership potential, and cultural fit with organizational values. The challenge was to balance technical competence demonstration with interpersonal skills while managing interview anxiety and maintaining authentic engagement throughout the session.

#### Action
In preparation for the mock interview, I invested substantial time reviewing commonly asked interview questions in software development contexts, studying both their technical content and effective strategies for responding to them clearly and confidently. I practiced rehearsing my responses using the STAR and CAR methods, which helped me structure answers in ways that were both comprehensive and concise, ensuring I covered relevant details without becoming verbose or losing focus. I carefully refined my responses to align closely with the projects, experiences, and technical skills highlighted in my CV and portfolio, ensuring consistency between my written materials and verbal presentation. During the actual mock interview session itself, I made conscious efforts to maintain professionalism in all aspects of my presentation, from my posture and eye contact to my tone of voice and energy level. I focused on articulating my thoughts clearly and methodically when answering technical questions, avoiding the temptation to rush through explanations or become flustered when faced with challenging problems. I also practiced active listening, ensuring I fully understood each question before responding and asking for clarification when needed rather than making assumptions that might lead to irrelevant answers.

#### Result
The mock interview experience proved tremendously valuable for identifying specific areas where I could improve my interview performance before facing actual employment opportunities. Through feedback from interviewers and self-reflection afterward, I recognized that I needed to work on refining my technical explanations to make them more accessible and well-structured, particularly when discussing complex programming concepts or architectural decisions. I also identified a tendency to use filler words like "um," "like," and "you know" when nervous or thinking through answers, and I've since worked consciously to minimize these verbal tics through practice and increased confidence. Perhaps most significantly, the mock interview experience substantially boosted my overall confidence in my ability to handle real interview settings effectively. By successfully navigating this realistic simulation, I proved to myself that I possess both the technical knowledge and communication skills necessary to compete for software development positions. The experience demystified the interview process, transforming it from an unknown and anxiety-inducing prospect into a familiar challenge that I can prepare for systematically. I now feel genuinely prepared to engage with potential employers in formal interview environments, confident that I can present myself professionally while authentically conveying my capabilities, interests, and potential contributions to their organizations.

The sessions examining workplace etiquette went far beyond superficial tips about office manners, instead delving into the underlying principles of professional behavior including how communication styles affect team dynamics, why punctuality and reliability build trust and credibility, how respect for diversity and inclusion strengthens organizations, and why emotional intelligence and interpersonal sensitivity are increasingly recognized as critical professional competencies alongside technical skills. We explored how workplace culture varies significantly across organizations and industries, and how successful professionals must develop the adaptability to understand and align with different organizational norms while maintaining personal integrity and authentic professional identity.

#### Task
My specific task within this workplace etiquette learning experience was to demonstrate comprehensive understanding of appropriate workplace behavior across multiple dimensions—including professional communication standards both written and verbal, the critical importance of punctuality and meeting commitments, effective teamwork and collaboration practices, and demonstrating genuine respect for diversity in all its forms including cultural, generational, and cognitive differences. The objective was to ensure that I could adapt seamlessly into any professional environment, regardless of its specific organizational culture or industry norms, by internalizing fundamental principles of professionalism that transcend particular workplace contexts. This required developing not just knowledge about workplace etiquette rules but genuine understanding of why these practices matter and how they contribute to creating positive, productive, and respectful work environments where all team members can thrive.

#### Action
I applied principles of professionalism systematically by practicing proper communication etiquette in all my interactions, paying careful attention to both written communications like emails, instant messages, and documentation, as well as verbal communications including meetings, presentations, and informal conversations. I learned to consider my audience, purpose, and context when choosing communication channels and crafting messages, understanding that different situations require different levels of formality and different communication approaches. I made punctuality a non-negotiable personal standard, recognizing that consistently arriving on time to meetings, meeting deadlines for deliverables, and respecting others' time demonstrates reliability and professionalism that builds trust over time. I practiced being respectful of professional boundaries, understanding when informal interaction was appropriate versus when more formal behavior was expected, and learning to read social cues that indicated which mode was suitable for different contexts. I also consciously worked to be respectful of cultural differences, recognizing that colleagues from different backgrounds might have different communication styles, work preferences, and cultural norms that deserved understanding and accommodation rather than judgment. Throughout my work-integrated learning experiences, I carefully observed how effective leadership and positive collaboration dynamics actually function in real workplace scenarios, noting how respected professionals balanced authority with approachability, provided constructive feedback without undermining confidence, resolved conflicts diplomatically, and created inclusive environments where diverse perspectives were valued. I made accountability and teamwork central to my professional identity, taking ownership of my responsibilities, supporting colleagues proactively, and contributing constructively to team objectives rather than focusing solely on individual achievement.

#### Result
Through this focused attention on workplace etiquette and professionalism, I learned that maintaining consistent professionalism and cultivating emotional intelligence within workplace interactions are fundamental to building trust among colleagues, establishing credibility with supervisors and clients, and fostering positive organizational culture that benefits everyone. I discovered that professionalism isn't about being stiff, formal, or inauthentic; rather, it's about being respectful, reliable, and thoughtful in how we interact with others, recognizing that our behavior impacts not just our own career trajectory but also the work experience of our colleagues and the overall effectiveness of our teams. I gained practical understanding that small behaviors—like responding to messages promptly, preparing thoroughly for meetings, giving colleagues full attention during conversations, and acknowledging others' contributions—accumulate over time to create professional reputation and trust that become invaluable career assets. This comprehensive reflection on workplace etiquette has prepared me to enter professional spaces with the right mindset, behavioral norms, and interpersonal skills necessary for success. I now feel confident that I can navigate various professional environments effectively, adapting appropriately to different organizational cultures while maintaining my authentic professional identity. I understand that professionalism is not a static achievement but an ongoing practice requiring continuous self-awareness, reflection, and refinement as I encounter new situations, challenges, and workplace dynamics throughout my career. Most importantly, I've internalized that demonstrating respect, reliability, and emotional intelligence in all professional interactions will enable me to contribute effectively and build meaningful professional relationships in any organization I join, establishing a foundation for long-term career success and personal fulfillment in the software development field.
