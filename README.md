## Creating a CV

### 📂 Evidence
- Link to CV Markdown on GitHub
- ![CV preview](/images/CV_sub_image.png)
- ![CV preview 2](/T4_Digital_Portfolio/images/CV_sub_image_2.png)

### 💭 Reflection (STAR Technique)

#### Situation
For my Project Presentation module we were tasked with creating a professional CV tailored to the tech industry. As part of this formative assessment ('Create a CV') we were briefed on:
- The primary purposes of a CV
- How to structure a CV appropriately
- What information should be included (experience, education, contact details, etc.)

#### Task
I needed to highlight all my skills, projects, and education in a way that clearly aligns with the jobs that were available.

#### Action
I used a professional Canva template that was easy to customize, producing a clean, modern CV. I also used GitHub and Markdown to create README files and a portfolio that demonstrate competency to recruiters.

#### Reflection / Result
I am now more confident that my CV effectively showcases my potential and is structured so it can be kept up to date and relevant to software engineering roles.

## Mock Interview

### 📂 Evidence
- ![Mock interview](/T4_Digital_Portfolio/images/image-5.png)

### 💭 Reflection (STAR Technique)

#### Situation
As part of our Professional Practice 3 module, we participated in a mock interview session designed to simulate a real hiring process. The purpose was to prepare us for professional interviews by developing our communication, confidence, and ability to articulate our strengths effectively.

#### Task
My task was to treat this as a real interview — to prepare by researching the company and role, dress appropriately, and be ready to answer both technical and behavioural questions that would typically come up during an interview for a software development position.

#### Action
I prepared by reviewing common interview questions, rehearsing my responses using the STAR and CAR methods, and refining my answers to align with my CV and portfolio projects. During the session, I focused on maintaining professionalism, clear articulation, and confident body language while addressing both technical and personal questions.

#### Result
The experience allowed me to identify areas where I could improve — such as refining my technical explanations and reducing filler words. It significantly boosted my confidence in handling real interview settings, and I now feel more prepared to engage with potential employers in a formal interview environment.
